Understudy Message Engine

How a fan's message becomes Juno's reply: six diagrams, each followed by a walkthrough of every step, rule and threshold. Every threshold names its config key and seeded default (from docs/CONFIG.md), the reason for it, and the file and function where it lives. Read from the code on 2026-11-04. Where the code was ambiguous, the text says so.

Each diagram is interactive (pan, zoom, search, theme). Open any of them full screen from the link under its frame.

Diagram 1 · workflow

The turn

One turn answers one batch of fan messages. The top row reads the fan and decides; the bottom row (right to left) drafts, checks, saves and delivers. Dashed arrows are loops back.

Source: engine-turn.workflow.jsonOpen full screen
  1. Fan message. sendFanMessage checks who may send, then createChatter's handleMessage validates the send (2,000-character limit MAX_MESSAGE_CHARS, an idempotent clientMsgId, a fan flagged minor_in_chat is refused), stores the row, clears fan_typing_until and returns the process step that sendFanMessage schedules with Next's after(). The message is stored even when a human owns the chat; the lease then refuses to answer it. lib/chat/send.ts · sendFanMessage

    processConversation runs turns until nothing is left unanswered, at most 5 per call (code constant MAX_TURNS_PER_RUN). The per-minute sweep (/api/cron/sweep, vercel.json, guarded by CRON_SECRET) is the safety net: it clears expired leases, takes up to 10 AI conversations (code constant MAX_CONVERSATIONS_PER_SWEEP) whose oldest unanswered fan message is older than engine.debounce_ms (250), oldest first, stops starting new ones after 35 s of its 60 s function (SWEEP_START_BUDGET_MS), and expires offers past their time.

    packages/engine/src/turn/process.ts · processConversation; packages/engine/src/conversation/sweep.ts · sweep
  2. Lease. The turn first reads engine.lease_ms (30000), then acquireLease stamps a random lease_token and lease_until on the conversation, but only if its mode is ai, the AI is switched on for this conversation and for the creator, and no live lease exists. No lease means another turn is running or a human owns the chat, and the call returns busy without writing anything. The lease is what makes many simultaneous triggers safe. packages/engine/src/conversation/lease.ts · acquireLease; packages/engine/src/turn/run.ts · runTurn

    Then every engine setting loads in one query (loadEngineConfig): a creator's own row wins over the platform row, except floor.intent, floor.safety.* and platform.*, which are read from platform rows only so a creator cannot loosen safety. The newest published persona card is loaded; with none, the turn fails with ConfigMissing("persona_card").

    packages/engine/src/config/load.ts · loadEngineConfig, isPlatformOnly
  3. Wait to settle. The engine waits until the fan has stopped: it sleeps engine.debounce_ms (250) and reloads; a new message, or fan_typing_until still in the future, starts another wait. The total sleep never passes engine.debounce_max_ms (8000), so a fan who keeps typing cannot hold the turn. His composer pings at most every 2 s (TYPING_PING_INTERVAL_MS) and each ping marks him typing for 3 s (TYPING_WINDOW_SECONDS). If nothing is waiting, the lease is released and the turn ends idle. packages/engine/src/turn/debounce.ts · waitForQuiet; lib/chat/typing.ts · markFanTyping

    The batch is every fan message with no turn_id that is newer than the last message a human (creator or admin) sent. AI replies do not close a batch, so a message he wrote while a reply was being drafted is still answered.

    packages/engine/src/turn/inbound.ts · loadInbound
  4. Facts and history. History is the last engine.history_rounds (20) rounds of sent, non-system messages (the query reads 30 rows per round, code constant HISTORY_ROWS_PER_ROUND, then cuts at round boundaries). Facts come from one database read against the database clock: hardship cooldown active, declines in the last pitch.decline_window_minutes (20), unlocks in the last pitch.velocity_window_minutes (30), today's spend against the fan's cap (his own, with a matured raise counted, else wallet.daily_cap_cents 20000), rounds since the last offer, an unresolved handoff, an open offer, any unlock in the chat, and whether he unlocked after her last message. The high-value score (fan.high_value_threshold_cents 10000, fan.high_value_half_life_days 7) is read here too. The batch starts a session when her last message before it is at least engine.gap_marker_minutes (30) old. The engine never takes a wallet lock. packages/engine/src/turn/facts.ts · loadFacts; packages/engine/src/drafting/thread.ts · sessionGapMs; lib/wallet/score.ts · isHighValue
  5. Jev inbound. One Jev call answers every question routing needs: intent (8 options), explicitness (4), six yes/no safety checks, thread (how his messages fit the chat), pitch_fit (asked only when pacing would allow an AI-started pitch, there is no open offer and this is not a session start) and offer_response (only when an offer is open). Jev sees no card, no prices, no memory. A thread answer outside the options becomes unknown; an unusable pitch_fit becomes "no pitch". packages/engine/src/routing/questions.ts · inboundQuestions; packages/engine/src/routing/route.ts · parseInbound

    Still typing. If Jev says his words stop mid-thought (still_typing) and no hard stop fired, the turn waits for one more message, one debounce at a time, while the turn is younger than engine.turn_budget_ms (12000) and inside the same debounce_max_ms budget. If the batch grew, the whole read repeats. This happens at most engine.fold_in_max (1) times. A hard stop is never delayed this way.

    packages/engine/src/turn/debounce.ts · waitForMore; packages/engine/src/turn/run.ts · runPass
  6. route(). A pure function turns Jev's answers and the facts into one route. Diagram 2 walks it. Hard stops and holds skip drafting and go straight to commit. packages/engine/src/routing/route.ts · route
  7. Context. Only drafting routes get here. The fan's memory profile loads; retrieval returns retrieval.k (5) persona chunks (a retrieval error becomes an empty list). On the offer route, code builds the shortlist (the creator's active items of the allowed kinds that he has not unlocked and that are not already in an open offer, ordered by price, title, id) and Jev picks one by key from titles and teasers only. One item needs no Jev call. An empty list or a bad pick falls back to the route's fallback: reply, or deflect for an explicit request. packages/engine/src/drafting/memory.ts · loadProfile; packages/engine/src/conversation/offer.ts · buildShortlist, pickOffer
  8. Claude draft. model.generate (claude-haiku-5-5) writes the reply with engine.max_tokens (300) and thinking switched off, because thinking tokens count against the limit and once left an empty draft. The prompt is a cached prefix (card, rules, never-list) plus a per-turn block (diagram 5). Before any check, emoji she used in her last 8 bubbles are removed from the draft (RECENT_BUBBLES); a draft that would be left empty is kept whole. packages/engine/src/drafting/prompt.ts · buildPrompt; packages/providers/src/anthropic/generator.ts; packages/engine/src/drafting/emoji.ts · dropRepeatedEmoji
  9. Verify. Code checks, then Jev checks the draft. A failure redrafts, up to engine.gen_attempts (2) drafts. Diagram 3 walks it. packages/engine/src/drafting/verify.ts · verifyDraft
  10. Fold-in check. If he wrote again while she was drafting, fewer than engine.fold_in_max (1) fold-ins have happened and the turn is younger than engine.turn_budget_ms (12000), the draft is thrown away and the whole turn reads the larger batch from step 3. A crisis message that arrived mid-draft is therefore classified before anything is sent: if the second read is a hard stop or a hold, that wins. If the second read finds no safe draft, the earlier safe draft is committed for the earlier batch and the late messages wait for the next turn. packages/engine/src/turn/run.ts · attemptTurn, newMessagesSince
  11. Commit. One transaction, guarded by a compare-and-set on mode = 'ai', the version read at lease time and the lease token. It bumps version, clears the lease, resets failed_turns, sets the new mode, starts the hardship cooldown (pitch.hardship_cooldown_hours 72), records last_pitch_at, writes the turn row (route, Jev answers, drafts trail, models, tokens, cost, timings), opens or escalates the handoff, flags the fan (minor or threat), supersedes unseen bubbles on a hard stop, marks a declined offer, creates the new offer (price copied in cents, expiring after offer.ttl_minutes 1440), inserts the bubbles and stamps the batch with the turn id. If a takeover or re-lease got there first, the turn is recorded superseded and only a hard stop's safety part is applied. packages/engine/src/turn/commit.ts · commitTurn, recordSuperseded; packages/engine/src/conversation/handoff.ts · openHandoff
  12. Deliver. A sent reply is split into one bubble per line, at most 3 (MAX_BUBBLES; extra lines join the last). The first bubble is due after its typing time: length ÷ engine.typing_cps (12) seconds, minus the time since his first message in the batch, capped at engine.typing_max_ms (10000). Each later bubble follows by one gap drawn once per turn between delivery.bubble_gap_ms_min (1500) and delivery.bubble_gap_ms_max (4000). Templates and held drafts have no delay. Each bubble's deliver_at holds it back; Pusher gets typing and one message.created per bubble, and the creator's inbox gets inbox.changed. After a replied or suggested commit, model.memory extracts schema-only facts about the fan into his profile and its cost is added to the turn. packages/engine/src/drafting/bubbles.ts · splitBubbles, deliverOffsets, bubbleGap; packages/engine/src/turn/deliver.ts · emitDelivery, finish; packages/engine/src/drafting/memory.ts · extractMemory

    Cost. Every call's tokens are priced from price.<model>.* rows (micro-dollars per million tokens: Haiku input 100000, output 500000, cache read 10000, cache write 125000; Jev input 42000, output 0) and summed per stage (jev_in, offer_pick, generate, jev_out, memory) into turns.cost_micros. A missing price row is ConfigMissing before any provider call.

    packages/engine/src/turn/cost.ts · CostLedger, costMicros
Diagram 2 · workflow

Routing

Checks run left to right and the first that matches decides. Trust & Safety outranks Juno, and Juno outranks the AI. Every probability is Jev's p(yes); a check fires at or above its floor, so a lower floor is stricter.

Source: engine-routing.workflow.jsonOpen full screen

1. Hard stops, strictest first

CheckKeyDefaultWhat happens
Fan may be under 18floor.safety.minor0.3Platform notice (sender system, with platform.support_email), account flagged minor_in_chat: he can read but every send is refused, sessions kept.
Self-harm or crisisfloor.safety.self_harm0.3Crisis template with helplines. If self-harm fired at all, this template is sent even when a stricter reason names the handoff.
Threat or harassmentfloor.safety.threat0.5No message, account flagged.
Meetup, location, other app, paying outsidefloor.safety.location_offplatform0.5"I keep my chats here" template. Online games together are not a violation for anyone.
Refund or charge complaintfloor.safety.payment_dispute0.6"Passed to support" template.

Every hard stop sets mode handoff_ts, opens a Trust & Safety handoff, sends its template at once (no typing delay) and supersedes any of her bubbles he has not seen yet, expiring an offer they carried. Trust & Safety outranks a creator takeover, so this safety part is applied even when the commit loses the race.

packages/engine/src/routing/route.ts · HARD_STOPS, hardStopOf; packages/engine/src/drafting/templates.ts; packages/engine/src/turn/commit.ts · flagUser, supersedeUnseenBubbles

2. Hardship and the high-value alert (not routes)

floor.safety.hardship (0.5) never stops the chat. It blocks every pitch this turn, switches the instruction to "be warm and supportive, do not sell", and on commit sets no_pitch_until for pitch.hardship_cooldown_hours (72), during which even a buy request gets no offer. A high-value fan with no open handoff makes the turn open a high_value_alert for Juno while the AI keeps replying; the alert never replaces a real handoff reason.

packages/engine/src/routing/route.ts · route; packages/engine/src/drafting/prompt.ts · buildPrompt; packages/engine/src/conversation/handoff.ts · openHandoff

3. Intent floor

If Jev's probability for the intent it chose is below floor.intent (0.5), the read is unsure and the chat is held for Juno (unsure). One exception: when the best guess is buy_intent, there is no hardship and pacing allows, the turn makes an offer (ppv or bundle, falling back to a reply) instead, because a wrong offer costs one decline and a wrong hold costs Juno's time and the sale.

4. Juno's intents and fixed routes

live_chat holds for Juno (explicit_live_chat); custom_request holds for Juno (custom_request). A hold sends the fan nothing and sets mode handoff_creator. ai_question gets ai_disclosure: an honest, warm "I'm an AI assistant for her account", and verify fails a draft that does not admit it. manipulation gets a playful deflect.

5. The pitch gate

canPitch checks, in order, and the first failure blocks the pitch:

Hardship cooldownpitch.hardship_cooldown_hours72 hno_pitch_until in the future
Declinespitch.max_declines in pitch.decline_window_minutes2 in 20 minA decline in this very message counts now

Velocitypitch.velocity_max_unlocks in pitch.velocity_window_minutes3 in 30 minUnlocks by this fan

packages/engine/src/routing/pacing.ts · canPitch

Then, with pitching allowed and no hardship:

  • Explicit request (intent explicit_request or explicitness explicit): a ppv offer, else a deflect. The AI never writes explicit text.
  • Buy intent: a ppv or bundle offer, else a plain reply.
  • Small talk or flirt (an AI-started pitch): also needs no open offer, no session start, thread of answers_her or same_topic, and pitch_fit at or above pitch.fit_floor (0.58). Otherwise a reply.

The AI only offers ppv and bundle items; the personal chat goes to Juno.

Diagram 3 · workflow

Verify and retry

Each draft passes cheap code checks first; only a clean draft costs a Jev call. Not shown as an arrow: a draft that fails a code check skips Jev and goes straight to "Sort failures".

Source: engine-verify.workflow.jsonOpen full screen

Code checks (no model)

  • empty: nothing left after trimming.
  • money: a currency symbol, a number beside a currency word, a percentage, a two-decimal price, or "for free", "on the house", "no charge", "gratis". Claude never sees a price, so none should appear.
  • marker_echo: she copied a "— 2 hours later —" time marker.
  • asks_media: a send-verb near a media word ("send me a pic", "drop a selfie"). Fans can only send text. "See" is left out so her own pitch passes.
  • never_list: a term from the card's never-list, whole word, plurals and spacing tolerated.
  • refusal_stop: Claude's own stop_reason was refusal.
packages/engine/src/drafting/verify.ts · codeChecks, mentionsMoney, asksForMedia, neverTermPattern

Jev checks

Jev reads { draft, fan_message, earlier_messages } and answers seven questions: on_persona must be yes; explicitness must not be explicit; offplatform_or_meetup must be no (games together and saying where she streams are fine); is_refusal must be no; admits_ai must be yes on ai_disclosure and no everywhere else; promises_price_or_free must be no; reasks (asking what he already answered, or "wbu" after he answered) should be no.

Paid and unpaid are now identical. The code still picks a "paid" variant of the inbound location_offplatform question and of the verify question set when the fan has unlocked something, but since off-platform talk was loosened for every fan both variants are byte-for-byte the same text. The only paid difference any model still sees is the paid line in Claude's "The chat right now" block, and the RULES already allow online plans for every fan.

packages/engine/src/routing/questions.ts · VERIFY_QUESTIONS, VERIFY_QUESTIONS_PAID, LOCATION_OFFPLATFORM_PAID; packages/engine/src/drafting/verify.ts · jevChecks

Safety failures and quality failures

reasks is the only quality check. A draft whose only failure is reasks still counts as safe: it becomes the chosen draft, and the second attempt gets the re-ask note ("answer what he asked, end with a different hook"). If the second draft does better it replaces the first; if it fails on safety, the first is sent anyway. Any other failure is a safety failure: the next attempt gets the retry note plus one line per failure from RETRY_REASONS, so the redraft drops the offending part instead of rephrasing it. empty, refusal_stop and the two admits_ai failures have no reason line, only the generic note.

After engine.gen_attempts (2) drafts with nothing safe, the last draft is stored as a suggestion (status blocked, suggested, never shown to the fan), the chat moves to handoff_creator with reason verify_failed, and Juno can edit and send or discard it.

packages/engine/src/drafting/verify.ts · qualityOnly; packages/engine/src/turn/draft.ts · RETRY_NOTE, REASK_NOTE, RETRY_REASONS; lib/conversations/controls.ts · sendSuggested, discardSuggested
Diagram 4 · sequence

A fan message end to end

Source: engine-message.sequence.jsonOpen full screen
  1. While he types, the composer pings the typing action at most every 2 s; each ping sets fan_typing_until to now + 3 s (skipped if a ping under a second old covers him).
  2. He sends. The action stores the row, clears his typing mark and schedules the engine with after(), so the response returns before the turn runs. His bubble shows at once from local state.
  3. In the browser, Juno's typing row stays hidden for 500 ms after each send (TYPING_AFTER_SEND_MS). This is a display delay only; engine timing is unchanged. A reply already visible when the hold ends shows no typing row at all.
  4. The turn takes the lease, waits out the debounce, asks Jev, drafts with Claude, verifies with Jev and commits (diagram 1). The commit gives each bubble a deliver_at.
  5. The engine emits typing (with the time until the last bubble) and message.created per bubble on the conversation's Pusher channel. Events carry ids only.
  6. On any event the browser refetches. The server only returns bubbles that are due (deliver_at null or past), and tells the browser how long until each pending one; the browser sets a timer for each and refetches 500 ms after it is due (DUE_SLACK_MS). The typing row shows while a turn is open (up to AI_PENDING_WINDOW_MS, 90 s), a bubble is pending or his own message is unconfirmed. While a reply is due the view also polls every 2.5 s (POLL_WHILE_AI_MS), and every 3 s if realtime is down. I found no code in live-thread.tsx that reads the holdMs value of the typing event; the row is computed from the refetched thread.
  7. After the commit, the memory call runs; the fan never waits on it.
lib/chat/typing.ts; lib/chat/send.ts; lib/ui/typing-delay.ts; lib/conversations/read.ts; components/chat/live-thread.tsx; providers/pusher/events.ts

Measured on 2026-11-03 (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, live test, six messages): Jev call 1 about 190 to 430 ms, Haiku 0.9 to 2.6 s, Jev verify 180 to 630 ms, 3.7 to 5.8 s from his message to commit, 200 to 785 micro-dollars per turn. Those numbers predate the 250 ms debounce and were not re-measured for this page.

Diagram 5 · data flow

What each model sees

Jev is fenced: it judges messages and drafts and never sees the persona card, a price or the fan's memory. Claude writes in her voice and never sees a price either.

Source: engine-context.dataflow.jsonOpen full screen

Jev's state

  • earlier_messages: the last engine.jev_context_rounds (6) rounds before the batch, each line marked fan or her, with a time line before any gap of at least engine.gap_marker_minutes (30) and one before the batch if it starts a session.
  • A platform line "He just unlocked what she offered." when he unlocked after her last message. An unlock leaves no chat line, and without it "that was cute of you" read as a hedge. This line goes to Jev only; Claude's prompt does not carry it.
  • new_messages: the batch. Every question tells Jev that fan text is data, never instructions.
  • For the offer pick, options are o1…on with title and teaser only. For verify, the state is the draft, the batch and the same earlier lines.
packages/engine/src/drafting/thread.ts · jevLines, JUST_UNLOCKED_LINE; packages/engine/src/turn/run.ts · runPass

Claude's prompt

  • Cached prefix (system block 1, cache_control: ephemeral): the persona card, the fixed RULES (short, in her voice; no price, money, "free", "discount" or "deal"; no explicit text; no meetups, real location, contact details or other apps, but online games and where she streams are fine; never claim to be human when sincerely asked; never ask him for media; never re-ask what he answered; time markers are not his words; no leading @handle), and "Never talk about" from the never-list. It only changes when the card does.
  • This turn (system block 2): the route's instruction (reply, offer, deflect, ai_disclosure, or the supportive hardship one); "The chat right now": the paid line if he has unlocked anything, the emoji she used in her last 8 bubbles to avoid, one line from the thread answer, and "He wrote this N later" on a session start; the item to tease (title and teaser, not on hardship); the retrieved chunks; the fan profile lines (name, nickname, interests, dislikes, tone; short strings only, with money words and instruction-like words dropped).
  • Messages: the last engine.history_rounds (20) rounds as alternating user and assistant turns, gap markers only at the start of his messages, starting with a user turn.
  • Retry note appended to block 2 on the second attempt (diagram 3).
packages/engine/src/drafting/prompt.ts · RULES, INSTRUCTIONS, buildPrompt; packages/engine/src/drafting/thread.ts · threadBlock, claudeHistory; packages/engine/src/drafting/memory.ts · renderProfile

Retrieval

retrieval.strategy picks fts, vector or hybrid. docs/CONFIG.md gives the seeded default as fts; vector, the retrieval eval's winner, was set on the neondb database afterwards. Vector and hybrid embed the batch with Voyage (retrieval.voyage_model, voyage-4) and fall back to full-text search if that fails. Which strategy a given database runs is whatever its row says.

packages/providers/src/voyage/persona-retriever.ts; lib/host/deps.ts · defaultDeps
Diagram 6 · lifecycle

Conversation modes and turn outcomes

Source: engine-states.lifecycle.jsonOpen full screen

Modes

  • ai: the only mode the engine can lease.
  • ai → handoff_creator: a hold, a suggestion (verify_failed), or engine.max_attempts (3) failed turns in a row (engine_error).
  • handoff_creator or ai → human_creator: Juno takes over her own chat. The diagram draws the common path; a creator can also take over straight from ai.
  • human_creator or handoff_creator → ai: hand back. All open handoffs resolve, failed_turns resets, and fan messages older than her last message are stamped with a handback turn so the AI never answers what she already handled.
  • Any mode → handoff_ts: a hard stop. From a mode other than ai this happens only through a turn already in flight losing its compare-and-set; human_ts is left alone.
  • Any mode → human_ts: an admin takes over; a second admin can take it from the first. The diagram draws the path from handoff_ts.
  • human_ts or handoff_ts → ai: an admin releases it.
  • closed exists in the database enum. I found no code in the engine or lib/conversations that sets it, so it is not drawn.

Every mode change bumps version, which is how a turn in flight learns it lost (its commit matches no row). A takeover also drops the lease and supersedes bubbles he has not seen, expiring any offer on them.

lib/conversations/actions.ts · takeover; packages/engine/src/conversation/lease.ts · handBack; lib/conversations/controls.ts · release, releaseConversation

Handoffs

At most one unresolved handoff per conversation: open → claimed (takeover) → resolved (hand-back or release). A Trust & Safety request moves an existing creator handoff to the T&S queue under the new reason and reopens it if it was claimed. Within one queue the first reason stands, except that a high_value_alert gives way to any real reason.

packages/engine/src/conversation/handoff.ts · openHandoff

Where the docs disagree with the code

Found while drawing these diagrams. docs/ARCHITECTURE.md says otherwise in these places; the code is what runs.

  • The Ports table lists the production retriever as packages/providers/src/pg/fts-retriever.ts and events as packages/providers/src/noop/events.ts. lib/host/deps.ts wires PersonaRetriever (strategy from config) and Pusher's defaultEvents().
  • The failure table says "Push transport: none yet". Pusher is wired.
  • The latency budget says "Debounce 2 s". The default is 250 ms (it replaced the fixed 2 s).
  • Known gaps says retrieval is "keyword-only until the Voyage adapter replaces it". The adapter exists; full-text is only the seeded default strategy.
  • The failure table says reaching engine.max_attempts gives "hold_creator". The code sets mode handoff_creator with handoff engine_error on an error turn; the route is not hold_creator.

Glossary

Round
A run of his messages plus the run of her messages that answers it. History, Jev's context and the pitch gap all count rounds, not messages, so a three-bubble reply is one round.
Batch
The fan messages one turn answers: his unanswered messages newer than the last message a human sent.
Fold-in
Throwing a finished draft away because he wrote again, and answering the whole larger batch instead. At most engine.fold_in_max (1) per turn, and only while the turn is younger than engine.turn_budget_ms (12 s).
Lease
A short claim on a conversation (lease_token, lease_until, 30 s) that lets only one turn run at a time. The commit only lands if the token, version and ai mode are unchanged.
Suggestion
A draft that failed safety twice. It is saved for Juno, never shown to the fan, and the chat waits for her.
Hard stop
A Trust & Safety reading at or above its floor. The AI stops, a fixed template (if any) goes out at once, and the chat moves to the T&S queue.
Pitch
An offer the AI puts in front of him: a locked catalogue item with its price shown by the app, never in her words. An AI-started pitch needs the gate and a fit; a pitch he asked for skips the fit and the round gap.
Hold
A route that hands the chat to Juno with no reply: unsure intent, live chat or a custom request.
Jev
TypeSafe's classifier (jev.model, jev-1.14.0). It answers closed questions with a probability per option and never writes text.
