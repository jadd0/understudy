## How the system fits together

1. The creator's raw posts and replies (synthetic for Juno; a real scrape would stay out of git) are
   cleaned into a committed, anonymised bank (`data/persona/bank/`).
2. The bank is split 60/20/20 by post; a script drafts a persona card from the train split and
   scores it with Jev on the validation split. The test split is kept for a blind test.
3. The bank's chunks are loaded into pgvector next, with a retrieval eval deciding whether
   Voyage beats Postgres full-text.
4. Fans sign in with an emailed one-tap link behind Cloudflare Turnstile, on Auth.js; fans confirm 18+
   before chat. Juno and Trust & Safety sign in to shared demo seats.
5. Server-side guards send each role to its own area; a wrong role gets a 404 (`examples/app/lib/auth/guards.ts`).
6. One Neon Postgres holds identity, persona, conversation and money tables (`examples/app/db/schema.ts`).
7. Each fan message goes through Jev (classify), code (route), Haiku (draft), Jev (check); offers and
   prices are chosen by code, never the model.
8. Fans top up a wallet through Stripe; a signature-checked webhook credits an append-only ledger and
   unlocks debit under a row lock.
9. Humans take over through two queues, Juno and Trust & Safety; the strictest owner wins.
10. Every change passes `bash scripts/gate.sh`; the gate is the only proof (AGENTS.md). `npm run check` runs every check.
